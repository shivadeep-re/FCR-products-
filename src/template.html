How NEM and AEM are modelled

  • Thresholds are minutes of full FCR-N activation left: stored energy ÷ C for discharging, free room ÷ C for charging.
  • NEM switches on when either side drops below 30 min and off again above 57.5 min. It is only allowed while frequency is within 49.9–50.1 Hz. Its signal (−1, 0 or +1) is averaged over the last 300 s at 1 s steps, so it ramps in over 5 minutes. NEM power is 0.34 × C × that average, added to the FCR-N response.
  • AEM switches on at 5 min or less left on either side, at any frequency, and off above 10 min. In AEM the battery responds around the 5-minute average frequency instead of 50 Hz and counts as unavailable. NEM keeps running in parallel.
  • Sizing rules: battery power ≥ 1.34 × C and at least 1 h × C of energy in each direction, so energy ≥ 2 h × C.

Sources: ENTSO-E, Technical Requirements for FCR Provision in the Nordic Synchronous Area v1.1 (28 Mar 2025), section 3.5 and Table 10; Energinet, Prequalification of FCR-N and FCR-D (NEM and AEM thresholds and formulas). Frequency: Fingrid 0.1 s data, file __DATE_ISO__, times as given in the file. FCR-N response is linear with full output at ±100 mHz and no deadband. SOC is limited to 0–100 % and losses are split evenly between charging and discharging. Bid size changes by hour are not modelled.
