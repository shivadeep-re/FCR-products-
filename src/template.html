State of charge, %

Power: shaded bands mark AEM (pink), empty (orange) and full (blue). Frequency: the teal zone is FCR-N's active band and the amber zones are where FCR-D responds. SOC: the left axis is the battery's SOC; the right axis shows how many SOC points each product has moved since midnight (below zero = drained, above = charged).

Products

  • FCR-N: P = CN × (50 − f) / 0.1 Hz, limited to ±CN. Linear, full output at 49.9 and 50.1 Hz, no deadband.
  • FCR-D up: discharges below 49.9 Hz, rising linearly to full CD↑ at 49.5 Hz. FCR-D down: charges above 50.1 Hz, rising linearly to full CD↓ at 50.5 Hz. Modelled as a static response, without the dynamic ramp-up of the real requirement.
  • When products run together their responses are added and the total is limited by the battery's rated power. The response figures are each product's own energy (charge plus discharge); they can add up to more than the battery throughput because NEM and FCR-N partly cancel out.
  • SOC used by each product is its net energy since midnight as a share of battery capacity, without losses. Losses & limits is what remains: conversion losses, plus any response the battery could not deliver because it was empty or full.

Sizing check

  • Power upwards ≥ 1.34 CN + CD↑ + 0.2 CD↓, and downwards ≥ 1.34 CN + CD↓ + 0.2 CD↑. The 0.34 and 0.2 shares are reserved for NEM.
  • Energy: FCR-N needs 1 h × CN in each direction, FCR-D needs 20 min × CD in its own direction. When products are combined, the requirements are added up (my assumption; the source does not spell out combined bids).

Energy management (NEM and AEM)

  • Thresholds are minutes of full activation left. The FCR-D 20-minute energy is held back first, and FCR-N's thresholds only count the energy above it (my assumption for sharing one reservoir).
  • FCR-N NEM: on below 30 min and off above 57.5 min; power 0.34 × CN. FCR-D NEM: on below 20 min and off above 20 min; power 0.2 × CD, restoring the direction FCR-D uses. NEM only runs while 49.9 < f < 50.1 Hz and is averaged over 300 s at 1 s steps.
  • AEM, per product: on at 5 min or less left, at any frequency, and off above 10 min. The product then responds around the 5-minute average frequency instead of 50 Hz and counts as unavailable. NEM keeps running alongside.

Sources: ENTSO-E, Technical Requirements for FCR Provision in the Nordic Synchronous Area v1.1 (28 Mar 2025), section 3.5 and Table 10; Energinet, Prequalification of FCR-N and FCR-D (NEM and AEM thresholds, power shares and energy reservations). Frequency: Fingrid 0.1 s data, times as given in the file (built-in day: __DATE_ISO__). Values outside 49–51 Hz and single-sample spikes are removed and gaps filled with the previous value. SOC is limited to 0–100 %, and losses are split evenly between charging and discharging. Hourly bid changes are not modelled.
